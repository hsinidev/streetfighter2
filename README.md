Street Fighter 2: A Comprehensive Guide



Game Overview


The original Street Fighter 2 was released in 1991 for arcades and later ported to various platforms, including home consoles and PCs. Developed by Capcom, this fighting game is widely considered one of the best of all time.



How to Play


To play Street Fighter 2, start by selecting your character and facing mode. The game is divided into matches, with each match lasting until you lose or a set amount of time has elapsed. During each round, players can perform special moves, combos, and counterattacks.



Controls



Player Controls:




Facing: Move the character up, down, left, or right by pressing the corresponding buttons (Up, Down, Left, Right)


Lip: Perform a special move (e.g., roundhouse kick) by pressing the "Lip" button (usually L1 or LP on PS3)

Shield: Block incoming attacks by holding the "Block" button (usually B or BP on PS3)


Back Jump: Perform a back jump to avoid an attack


Air Combo: Perform a quick series of punches and kicks






Objective


The objective of the game is to defeat your opponent by landing more hits than they do. The player with the most hits at the end of the match wins.
